If you need more than a minute

Urge Walk is not a crisis service and isn’t a substitute for professional care. If you’re in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US, 24/7). For substance use or mental health treatment referrals, call the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7). In an emergency, call 911.
