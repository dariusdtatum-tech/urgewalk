Premium

Premium isn’t available yet. It will be an optional extra on top of the free app. You’ll never need it to ride out an urge.

Free forever

Everything in Urge Walk is free to use, with no ads and no account. Anything on this list stays free.

  • Ride it out: Walk
  • Ride it out: Breathe
  • Ride it out: Ground
  • Logging urges (Just log it)
  • Day counters and milestones
  • The weekly Urge Map
  • Daily check-in
  • “How did it go?” follow-up
  • Your reasons
  • Trusted contacts
  • Crisis resources (988)
  • Undo after saving
  • Backup, restore and data export
  • App lock
  • Journal
  • Web tools: Breathe and Ground

We’ll never sell a way to restore your count. Your count is yours.

Help

Urge Walk is not a crisis service and isn’t a substitute for professional care. In the US, call or text 988 any time (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7). Elsewhere, findahelpline.com. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
