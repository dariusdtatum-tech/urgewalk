Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026 · Last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version: Urge Walk for iPhone keeps your information on your device. Nothing is collected or sent to us or anyone else, and we never sell it.

What the app stores

To work, Urge Walk saves a small amount of information only on your device, such as the habits you track, the milestones you reach, and your app preferences. It stays in the app’s own storage on your iPhone.

This version of the app does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location, or health data, and it does not use Face ID.

What we collect

Nothing. Urge Walk has no accounts and does not send your data to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and no third-party SDKs.

Community and Discord

Community is optional. Tapping “Join on Discord” opens Discord, a separate service run by Discord Inc. What you do there is governed by Discord’s own privacy policy and terms, not this one. Urge Walk shares nothing with Discord. It only opens the invite link.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We can’t, because we never receive it.

How long data is kept

Your data stays on your device until you delete the app, or until you erase it with an in-app reset if your version of the app includes one. We don’t hold a copy anywhere else.

Deleting your data

You can remove your data at any time. Deleting Urge Walk from your iPhone removes all of its data from your device. If your version of the app includes a reset option, using it also erases your data. Because we don’t hold a copy, we can’t restore it for you.

Backups

If the app offers a backup export, the file it creates is plain, unencrypted text. It is made on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it, so keep it somewhere private. Where you store it is governed by that service’s own privacy policy.

Age

Urge Walk is for adults 18 and older, and asks you to confirm your age during setup. We do not collect information from anyone.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts collecting data or asking for new permissions, we’ll update this page before that version is released.
