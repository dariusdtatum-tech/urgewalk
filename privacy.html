Urge Walk · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026 · Last updated: November 25, 2026

The short version: Personal recovery records stay on your iPhone and your Apple Watch, always (see Apple Watch, below). Nothing is sent to us. Nothing is collected or shared with anyone else, and we never sell it.

What the app stores

A fresh install starts empty. As you use Urge Walk, it saves the following only on your device:

  • The habits you track (a name, a start date, and any resets)
  • The urges you ride out: walks, breathing and grounding sessions, and quick logs, with times, how it went, and any note
  • Daily check-ins, and any tags you add to an urge (the Urge Map)
  • Journal entries (title, text, and an optional mood)
  • Your reasons: what you write, and any photo or voice note you add
  • Trusted contacts: up to three names and numbers, and the message you choose
  • Your setup answers, and your anchor if you write one (a sentence to come back to)
  • Money kept, if you turn it on: an amount for each urge and a goal
  • Milestones you reach
  • Your settings, such as walk length, Island light, sounds and reminders
  • A crisis number, only if you type one in yourself

The app asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can say no and keep using everything else:

  • Apple Health, only if you allow it. See Apple Health, below.
  • Contacts, if you pick a trusted person from your contacts. iOS shows its own contact picker, so the app does not get your address book. Only the name and number you choose are saved, on your phone.
  • Photos, if you choose a photo for Your reasons. iOS shows its own photo picker, so the app does not get your photo library. The photo you choose stays on your phone.
  • Microphone, if you record a voice note for Your reasons. It stays on your phone.
  • Face ID, if you turn on App lock, the Journal lock or the Your reasons lock. Face ID is handled by iOS; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • Notifications, for a daily check-in reminder, a check-in after an urge, or when a walk's time is up, if you turn them on.

It does not ask for your camera or location.

Apple Health

Apple Health is off unless you allow it, and iOS asks you first. Here is exactly what Urge Walk does with it:

  • On your iPhone: it reads your step count, to show daily steps when you turn Steps on. It writes nothing to Health.
  • On your Apple Watch: it reads the steps, distance and heart rate of a walk you take with the Watch, and saves that walk to Apple Health as a walking workout with those three. The Watch also reads your step count for Steps.

This stays in Apple Health and on your devices. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and never used for advertising or marketing. To change or turn off access at any time, open Settings, then Health, then Data Access & Devices, then Urge Walk.

Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app, it keeps your trackers, your saved number, your trusted contacts and your ridden-out count, and holds any urges you log there until your iPhone has them. Your iPhone and Watch share this over Apple’s own connection between your devices, not through a server of ours.

Widgets and Live Activity

If you add a widget or use the Live Activity, it shows a snapshot the app keeps in storage it shares with its widgets on the same device. It stays on your phone or Watch. The Live Activity is local only: nothing is pushed to it from a server.

What we collect

Nothing. Urge Walk has no accounts and does not send your data to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. It is built with Apple’s own frameworks, and none of them send your data to us.

Backups you make

If you choose Back up, the app makes one file and opens your iPhone’s share sheet. A copy of the file is also kept on this phone, in the app’s own storage (Reset app does this too, and so does Back up if the share sheet doesn’t open). Any other copy goes only where you save or send it, such as Files or iCloud Drive, and is then covered by that service’s own terms. The file holds your trackers, walks, check-ins, journal, written reasons, setup answers, milestones, trusted contacts (names and numbers), any support number you saved, Money kept, your walk settings and your chosen destination, so keep it private. Photos and voice notes stay on the phone and are not in the file.

Links you choose to open

The app only leaves itself when you tap something. Calling or texting a crisis line opens your phone’s own Phone or Messages app. “Find a helpline” opens findahelpline.com, and the Privacy, Terms, and Support links open this website. Nothing is opened or sent in the background.

Community and Discord

Community is optional. Tapping “Join on Discord” opens our Discord invite (discord.gg/AAgaXxk43C). Discord is a separate service, and once you leave the app, Discord’s own privacy policy and terms apply. Urge Walk shares nothing with Discord.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We can’t, because we never receive it.

How long data is kept

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or delete the app. There is no copy on a server and no account to close.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time, in either of two ways:

  • In the app, go to You, then On this phone, then Reset app. This erases everything Urge Walk stores, including any crisis number you saved, and returns the app to the way it was when first installed.
  • Delete Urge Walk from your iPhone. This removes all of its data as well.

Because we don’t hold a copy, we can’t restore deleted data for you.

Age

Urge Walk is for adults 18 and older, and asks you to confirm your age during setup. We do not collect information from anyone.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The domain is registered with Cloudflare, which also runs its DNS. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It remembers your Daylight or Evening and Motion choices in your own browser, and keeps the Walk, Breathe and Ground pages for offline use; none of this is sent anywhere. Links from this site to other sites don’t tell them you came from here. If you email us, we see your email address. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts collecting data or asking for new permissions, we’ll update this page before that version is released.

Contact

Questions? Email hello@urgewalk.app.

Help

Urge Walk is not a crisis service and isn’t a substitute for professional care. In the US, call or text 988 any time (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7). Elsewhere, findahelpline.com. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
