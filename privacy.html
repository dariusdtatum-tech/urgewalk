Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026 · Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version: The current version of Urge Walk keeps your information on your device. We don’t collect it, we don’t see it, and we never sell it.

What the app stores

To work, Urge Walk saves the following only on your device:

  • The habits you track (a name and a start date)
  • Journal entries and drafts (title, text, mood)
  • Every urge you ride out: walks, one-minute breathing sessions, and quick logs, with times, how it went, and any note
  • Milestones you’ve earned and app preferences (such as your usual walk length)
  • Your setup answers: that you confirmed you’re 18 or older, how often urges used to come up, what you want to improve, how important this is to you, how you see yourself, and your “why” in your own words
  • Trusted contacts, if you add them: up to 3 people (a name and phone number for each) and the note you choose to send them
  • Your reasons, if you add them: a few words, a photo you choose from your library, or a short voice note you record, plus whether the optional Face ID lock is on
  • If you turn it on: an optional estimate of time and money per urge, used for a “Time back” figure

None of this leaves your phone unless you export a backup yourself.

Trusted contacts and the “Text” button

You can add up to 3 people you trust. You type each name and number in yourself, or pick one person at a time with your iPhone’s own contact picker. Picking a person shares only that one contact with Urge Walk; the app does not read or copy the rest of your contacts list. Tapping “Text” opens your phone’s Messages app addressed to that person, with your chosen note filled in, so you can press Send. Urge Walk never sends anything by itself. Names, numbers, and your note are stored only on your device.

Your reasons, photos, and voice notes

If you add a photo, Urge Walk asks for access to your photo library so you can choose it, then keeps its own copy inside the app’s storage on your device. If you record a voice note, the app asks to use the microphone only while you record. Photos and voice notes are never uploaded anywhere. You can turn on an optional Face ID lock that keeps your reasons covered until you unlock them; Face ID is handled by your iPhone, and Urge Walk never sees or stores your face data.

What we collect

Nothing. The current version of Urge Walk has no accounts and does not send your data to us or to anyone else. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and it does not access your location or health data. It only sees a contact you pick yourself, a photo you choose, or a voice note you record, as described above.

Accounts in a future version

A future version of Urge Walk will offer optional user accounts, for things like backing up your data and moving to a new phone. The app will keep working without one. Before that version is released, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what an account stores, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. Until then, the app has no accounts and everything above stays on your device.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We can’t, because we never receive it.

Backups

You can choose to export a backup file. That file is created on your device and goes wherever you decide to save or share it (for example, Files or iCloud Drive). It includes your journal, your setup answers, your trusted contacts’ names and numbers, and any reasons written as text, so keep it somewhere private. Photos and voice notes are not included in the backup file. Where you store it is governed by that service’s own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete trackers, logged urges, and journal entries inside the app, and change or clear your setup answers in You → Redo setup. You can remove trusted contacts in You → Trusted contacts, and remove any reason (including its photo or voice note) in You → Your reasons. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because we don’t hold a copy, we can’t restore it for you, so export a backup if you want to keep it.

Age

Urge Walk is for adults 18 and older, and asks you to confirm your age during setup. We do not collect information from anyone.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, we’ll say so here before it does. In particular, this policy will be updated before Urge Walk adds optional accounts, Apple Health (HealthKit) or motion data, or optional paid subscriptions.
