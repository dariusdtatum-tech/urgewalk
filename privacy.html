Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026 · Last updated: October 13, 2026

The short version: The current version of Urge Walk keeps your information on your device. We don’t collect it, we don’t see it, and we never sell it.

What the app stores

To work, Urge Walk saves the following only on your device:

  • The habits you track (a name and a start date)
  • Journal entries and drafts (title, text, mood)
  • Every urge you ride out: walks, one-minute breathing sessions, and quick logs, with times, how it went, and any note
  • Milestones you’ve earned and app preferences (such as your usual walk length)
  • Your setup answers: that you confirmed you’re 18 or older, how often urges used to come up, what you want to improve, how important this is to you, how you see yourself, and your “why” in your own words
  • If you choose “Someone I trust”: that person’s first name and phone number, which you type in yourself
  • If you turn it on: an optional estimate of time and money per urge, used for a “Time back” figure

None of this leaves your phone unless you export a backup yourself.

The “Text” button

If you add someone you trust, the Ride it out screen shows a “Text <name>” button. Tapping it opens your phone’s own Messages app addressed to that number, with no message filled in. Urge Walk never sends anything by itself, and the name and number are stored only on your device. Urge Walk does not read your contacts list.

What we collect

Nothing. The current version of Urge Walk has no accounts and does not send your data to us or to anyone else. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and it does not access your location, contacts, or health data.

Accounts in a future version

A future version of Urge Walk will add user accounts (they may be optional or required). Before that version is released, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what an account stores, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. Until then, the app has no accounts and everything above stays on your device.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We can’t, because we never receive it.

Backups

You can choose to export a backup file. That file is created on your device and goes wherever you decide to save or share it (for example, Files or iCloud Drive). It includes your journal, your setup answers, and your trusted contact’s name and number, so keep it somewhere private. Where you store it is governed by that service’s own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete trackers, logged urges, and journal entries inside the app, and change or clear your setup answers (including a trusted contact) in You → Redo setup. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because we don’t hold a copy, we can’t restore it for you, so export a backup if you want to keep it.

Age

Urge Walk is for adults 18 and older, and asks you to confirm your age during setup. We do not collect information from anyone.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, we’ll say so here before it does. In particular, this policy will be updated before Urge Walk adds accounts, Apple Health (HealthKit) or motion data, or optional paid subscriptions.
