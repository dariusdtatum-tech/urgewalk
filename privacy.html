Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

The short version: Urge Walk keeps your information on your device. We don’t collect it, we don’t see it, and we never sell it.

What the app stores

To work, Urge Walk saves the following only on your device:

  • The habits you track (a name and a start date)
  • Your walks: start and end times, length, how the urge went, and any note you add
  • Journal entries and drafts (title, text, mood)
  • Milestones you’ve earned and app preferences (such as your usual walk length)

What we collect

Nothing. Urge Walk has no accounts and does not send your data to us or to anyone else. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and it does not access your location, contacts, or health data.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. We can’t, because we never receive it.

Backups

You can choose to export a backup file. That file is created on your device and goes wherever you decide to save or share it (for example, Files or iCloud Drive). It includes your journal, so keep it somewhere private. Where you store it is governed by that service’s own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete trackers, walks, and journal entries inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because we don’t hold a copy, we can’t restore it for you, so export a backup if you want to keep it.

Children

Urge Walk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and uses no analytics. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts collecting data, we’ll say so here before it does.
