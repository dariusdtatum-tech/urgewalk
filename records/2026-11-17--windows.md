# 2026-11-17: Website: the places as windows that open, with sound (proposed, not merged)

## Request

Handed over by the main session on Nov 17. It reports that the founder said "Hand it over" there after reviewing a private test page. That is a go-ahead to build on a branch and open a PR. Merging still waits for the founder's own "Merge" in the website session.

Founder's idea (verbatim, as relayed): "What if instead of the site having the visuals be fully expanded, they were placed within cards and when someone hovers or clicks the card the visual expands into a full looped experience, with sound?"

Founder on kev's "Unslopping a vibecoded website" video (verbatim, as relayed): "This will help take the site to the next level".

Test page (private, not in this repo): `urge-walk-notes-and-assets/07 Design Direction/2026-11-17 card loops test/index.html`.

## What changed (branch `site/windows-2026-11-17`, homepage only)

- **Reveal:** with motion on, the arrival opens as a small window and grows to the full screen (1.1 s). Then the words rise and the header fades in. It is CSS only.
- **Windows:** the five places after the arrival (Coast, Clearing, Path, Tide Pool, Shore) are windows in a zig-zag walk, each with its number, name, a line or two and "Step in".
  - Each window shows the first frame of its place's loop, in the current light.
  - With a mouse and motion on, pointing at a window plays its loop silently after 150 ms.
- **Stepping in:** a click opens that place's own section over the page. This differs from the test page, which showed a short text only.
  - The section grows from the window's rectangle (clip-path, 520 ms; 300 ms to close).
  - It keeps everything the place had: its words, its app phone, the breath, the four answers, the five senses.
  - The place's loop plays and its sound fades in over 1.5 s.
  - Controls: Sound on / off (remembered on this device), close (the ✕ and Escape), "Next: …", and "Need help now? 988".
  - The page behind can't be reached or scrolled while a place is open. Focus moves to close, and returns to the window afterwards.
- **Places menu** (screens wider than 760 px): a "Places" button in the homepage header lists the five places, each with a picture preview. Picking one scrolls to its window and opens it.
- **Arrow nudge:** the → on "Step in", "Next" and the menu rows moves 4 px on hover.
- **Unchanged:** the Outlook, the Cottage and Night stay full sections. Every other page is untouched, and the icon stays Sea Glass.

### Sound

- Four files copied from the app at `376b215` (`assets/sounds/`, about 5.1 MB together). A file loads only when its place is opened.

| Place | Sound |
|---|---|
| Coast | sheltered-bay |
| Clearing | skylark-hedgerow |
| Path | skylark-hedgerow |
| Tide Pool | rock-pool-laps |
| Shore | pebble-shore |

- The sound starts only from the click that opens a place. It never plays on hover or on page load.
- The fade runs through a Web Audio gain node, because iPhone ignores `audio.volume`.
- The service worker doesn't cache the sound files.
- The footer credit now reads "Sounds here and in the app".

### Fallbacks

- **No JavaScript:** none of this applies. The page reads as it does today, with five full sections.
- **Reduce Motion or Motion off:** the windows show, and a place opens at once with no grow. Nothing plays until the visitor presses "Play the loop". Sound still follows the Sound button.
- **Reveal:** skipped under Reduce Motion and Motion off.

### Code

- `index.html`: the windows section, `data-window` and `data-sound` on the five sections, the place controls, the Places menu.
- `site.css`: one new block at the end.
- `site.js`: the Windows block. The walk leaves out windowed places until they are opened, and `syncMotion` plays an opened place.
- `sw.js`: cache `uw-2026-11-17-r1`.
- `scripts/check-light-switch.cjs`: now checks the window pictures too.

## Open decisions for the founder before merge

1. **Design rules:** DESIGN-RULES says "Each place of the app is a full-screen photograph" and "never as a feature grid". Windows change that model for five places. A proposed rules note is in DESIGN-RULES, marked as waiting for his OK.
2. **Motion rules:** the rules allow transform and opacity only. The clip-path grow and the reveal are a new exception.
3. **Sound:** the rules say loops have "no audio". Sound is new, and it is on by default once a place is opened.
4. **Sound licence:** the Pixabay licence doesn't allow sounds to be "redistributed on their own". Here they play as part of the page, as the footage does, but each file has its own address. His call.
5. **Shared sound:** the Clearing and the Path share one sound. The app plays the sheltered bay for Walk by evening; here the Path keeps the skylarks in both lights.
6. **The Outlook and the Cottage** have no window: there is no sound for them, and the Outlook has no wide daylight loop.
7. **Words in the open place:** the open place shows the place's full section. The test page showed a short text and one button.
8. **The reveal** delays the homepage's words by about 1.5 s on every visit. The Ride it out button shows at once.

### The arrival (the onboarding screen)

- **The words** sit on the page's own colour: the Coast's deep shade, where the boat lands (`data-place="coast"`).
  - On wide screens: the heading on the left; the lede, the ask and "See how it works" on the right.
  - On phones: one column.
- **The card:** a screen-wide card below the words.
  - Its frame is the Coast's sand tone mixed 30% into that deep shade. It is warm against the blue sea, with a hairline in the tone.
  - Inside are the boat loop and the app's phone (it plays its own recording, as before).
  - 16:9 on wide screens (no taller than the screen), 4:5 on phones.
  - The card grows in from a small window as the page opens. The words rise at once (no 1.5 s wait any more, which settles decision 8).
- **Clicking the card or the phone** opens the footage, not the app, full screen.
  - It grows from the card (520 ms). The ✕ or Escape closes it.
  - "Need help now? 988" stays at the foot.
  - Focus moves to close and returns to the card. The page behind can't be reached, and the Ride it out button hides while it's open.
- **Fallbacks:**
  - Reduce Motion or Motion off: the card and the full screen show the still, with "Play the loop". Closing ends that play.
  - No JavaScript: the card shows the still and does nothing on click; the words are the same.
- **Legibility:** cream text on `#211a12` (the Coast's deep shade) is well above 4.5:1, with no photograph behind the words.
