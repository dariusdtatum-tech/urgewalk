Frequently asked questions

When is Urge Walk coming out?

It’s being polished for its App Store release. There’s no date yet; this site will be updated when it launches.

How do I start a walk?

Tap the wave button labeled “Ride it out” in the middle of the tab bar, then tap Walk. It starts right away at your usual length. You can also press and hold the button to start a walk without opening the sheet.

How do I change my walk length?

Go to the You tab and tap Walk length. Pick 5, 10, or 15 minutes, a custom length (1–120 minutes), or an open walk with no timer.

What if I can’t go for a walk right now?

In Ride it out, tap Breathe for a one-minute guided breathing exercise, or tap “Just log it” to record that you rode one out. Both count in your log.

What if my screen locks during a walk?

The timer is based on saved times, so it stays correct if your screen locks or you close the app. Reopening takes you back to your walk.

Does a “didn’t pass” check-in reset my clean days?

No. It just records how that one urge went. Your clean-day count only resets if you reset it yourself.

Can I track more than one habit?

Yes. Go to You → Trackers to add, edit, reset, or delete trackers and choose which one shows on Home.

Who is Urge Walk for?

Adults 18 and older. Setup asks you to confirm your age.

What does the “Text” button do?

In You → Trusted contacts, add up to 3 people: type a name and number, or pick someone with your iPhone’s contact picker. Tapping “Text” opens your own Messages app addressed to them, with your chosen note filled in so you can press Send. Urge Walk never sends anything by itself, and names and numbers stay on your phone.

Where is my data stored?

In the current test version, everything is stored on your device and nothing is sent to a server. The public version will add optional user accounts; the Privacy Policy will explain what an account stores and where before that version is released.

How do I back up or move my data?

Go to You → Backup & restore to export everything to one file, and restore it later. The file includes your journal, so keep it private.

Will Urge Walk cost money?

The core features are free, and they stay free. An optional Premium is planned but isn’t available yet. See what stays free forever.
