Support

Need help with Urge Walk? Email hello@urgewalk.app and I’ll get back to you as soon as I can.

Urge Walk is not a crisis service and does not replace professional care. If you’re in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US, 24/7). Outside the US? Find a helpline at findahelpline.com. To find treatment for drug, alcohol or mental health problems, call the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 (free, private, 24/7). In an emergency, call 911.

Common questions

When is Urge Walk coming out?

The app is almost ready for the App Store. I don’t have a date yet. When it comes out, I’ll update this page.

How do I start a walk?

Tap “Ride it out” on Home. Then tap Walk. Your walk starts right away, at your usual length. Want to pick the length first? Tap Explore, then Walk.

How do I change my walk length?

Tap Explore, then Walk. Drag the handle around the ring to set the minutes. You can pick from 1 to 60. Want a walk with no timer? Tap Options, then choose Open.

What if I can’t go for a walk right now?

Tap “Ride it out” on Home. Then pick one of these. Breathe is a short breathing exercise with a guide, about one minute. Ground helps you notice what is around you, one sense at a time. “Just log it” saves that you already rode out an urge. Each one is saved in your History.

What if my screen locks during a walk?

Your walk keeps the right time. The app does not count seconds in the background. It remembers when you started. So the time stays right if your screen locks or you close the app. Open the app again and you’re back in your walk.

Will tapping “Stayed” reset my clean days?

No. “Stayed” only saves how that urge went. It also offers you help, in case you want it. Your clean-day count goes back to 0 only if you reset it yourself.

Can I track more than one habit?

Yes. Tap You, then Progress. Tap Trackers. There you can add a tracker, change one, start a new count, or delete one. Tap “Show on Home” to pick which one you see on Home.

Who is Urge Walk for?

Adults, 18 and older. When you set up the app, it asks you to confirm your age.

What does the “Text” button do?

Tap You, then “Support lines and trusted contacts.” Tap Trusted contacts. You can add up to 3 people. Tap Add someone, then type a name and number, or choose from Contacts. When you tap Text, your own Messages app opens. It is already addressed to that person, with your note filled in. You tap Send. Urge Walk never sends a message by itself. Names and numbers stay on your phone. They go in a backup file only if you make one.

Where is my data stored?

Your records stay on your iPhone. The Apple Watch app, if you use it, keeps your trackers, your saved number, your trusted contacts and your ridden-out count. It also holds any urges you log on the watch until your iPhone has them. Nothing is sent to a server. There is no account. Community is a link to a Discord server. You don’t need an account in the app. Discord’s own rules apply there. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or move my data?

Tap You, then Data and privacy, then Backup & restore. This saves one file. The file has your trackers, walks, check-ins, journal, written reasons, setup answers, trusted contacts and Money kept. You can restore it later on the same screen. Photos and voice notes stay on your phone. They are not in the file. The file has your journal and your trusted contacts in it, so keep it private.

Will Urge Walk cost money?

Everything in the app is free today. There are no ads and no account. I plan to offer an optional Premium later. It isn’t out yet. The price is to be announced. The core features will stay free.

I found a bug or have an idea. What do I do?

Please email hello@urgewalk.app. Your feedback helps me decide what to build next.
