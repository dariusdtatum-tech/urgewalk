Support

Need help with Urge Walk? Email hello@urgewalk.app and I’ll get back to you as soon as I can.

Urge Walk is not a crisis service and isn’t a substitute for professional care. If you’re in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US, 24/7). Outside the US? Find a helpline at findahelpline.com. For substance use or mental health treatment referrals, call the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7). In an emergency, call 911.

Frequently asked questions

When is Urge Walk coming out?

It’s being polished for its App Store release. There’s no date yet; this site will be updated when it launches.

How do I start a walk?

Tap “Ride it out” on Home, then tap Walk. It starts right away at your usual length. Or open Explore and choose Walk to pick a length first.

How do I change my walk length?

Open Walk and turn the ring to set the minutes, from 1 to 60. Under Options you can choose an open walk with no timer.

What if I can’t go for a walk right now?

In Ride it out, tap Breathe for a one-minute guided breathing exercise, Ground to come back to your senses one at a time, or “Just log it” to record that you rode one out. Each one counts in your log.

What if my screen locks during a walk?

The timer is based on saved times, so it stays correct if your screen locks or you close the app. Reopening takes you back to your walk.

Does a “stayed” check-in reset my clean days?

No. It just records how that one urge went, and offers help in case you want it. Your clean-day count only resets if you reset it yourself.

Can I track more than one habit?

Yes. Go to You, then Progress, then Trackers to add, edit, reset, or delete trackers and choose which one shows on Home.

Who is Urge Walk for?

Adults 18 and older. Setup asks you to confirm your age.

What does the “Text” button do?

In You, open Support lines and trusted contacts, and add up to 3 people: type a name and number, or pick someone with your iPhone’s contact picker. Tapping “Text” opens your own Messages app addressed to them, with your chosen note filled in so you can press Send. Urge Walk never sends anything by itself, and names and numbers stay on your phone, and in a backup file only if you make one.

Where is my data stored?

Your records are kept on your iPhone. If you use the Apple Watch app, it keeps your trackers, your saved number, your trusted contacts and your ridden-out count, and holds any urges you log there until your iPhone has them. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no account. Community is a link to a Discord server and needs no account in the app; Discord’s own terms apply there. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or move my data?

Go to You, then Data and privacy, then Backup & restore to save your trackers, walks, check-ins, journal, written reasons, setup answers, trusted contacts and Money kept to one file, and restore it later. Photos and voice notes stay on the phone and are not in the file. The file includes your journal and your trusted contacts, so keep it private.

Will Urge Walk cost money?

Everything in the app is free today, with no ads and no account. An optional Premium is planned but isn’t available yet, and the core features stay free.
