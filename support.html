Urge Walk is not a crisis service and isn’t a substitute for professional care. If you’re in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US, 24/7). For substance use or mental health treatment referrals, call the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7). In an emergency, call 911.

Frequently asked questions

When is Urge Walk coming out?

It’s being polished for its first App Store release. There’s no date yet; this site will be updated when it launches.

How do I start a walk?

Open the Walk tab and tap “I have an urge.” It starts right away at your usual length. Tap “Change length” to pick 5, 10, or 15 minutes, a custom length (1–120 minutes), or an open walk with no timer.

What if my screen locks during a walk?

The timer is based on saved times, so it stays correct if your screen locks or you close the app. Reopening takes you back to your walk.

Does a “didn’t pass” check-in reset my clean days?

No. It just records how that one urge went. Your clean-day count only resets if you reset it yourself.

Can I track more than one habit?

Yes. On Home, tap Edit to add, edit, reset, or delete trackers and choose which one shows on Home.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There’s no account and nothing is sent to a server. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I back up or move my data?

Go to Home → Edit → Backup & restore to export everything to one file, and restore it later. The file includes your journal, so keep it private.
