1. A wellness tool, not medical care

Urge Walk helps you build a habit: when an urge hits, take a short walk, breathe for a minute, come back to your senses, or log it. It is not medical, psychological, or addiction treatment, it doesn’t diagnose anything, and it isn’t a substitute for a doctor, counselor, or treatment program. Talk to a qualified professional about your health.

Urge Walk is not a crisis service. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US, 24/7). For substance use or mental health treatment referrals, call the SAMHSA National Helpline at 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7). In an emergency, call 911.

2. Age

You must be 18 or older to use Urge Walk. The app asks you to confirm your age during setup.

3. What’s free

The core features of Urge Walk, including riding out an urge (Walk, Breathe, Ground, and Just log it), your log, and your clean-day count, are free. Urge Walk shows no ads.
